Biography and practice summary are paraphrased. Artwork remains credited to Taylor Smalls and links to its original page. Images are retained locally as demo portfolio thumbnails. Age, financial need, sponsorship, qualifying exhibition counts, mature-practice years, residence requirements and proposed project facts remain unknown; no fictional applications or awards are seeded.

The personal store is artist-manager:v1; the demo store is artist-manager:demo:taylor-smalls:v1. A workspace indicator persists the chosen mode. Switching back restores the personal store and retains demo progress. A failed load/save must not replace personal work. Demo mode is labeled throughout the app and does not represent Taylor's own account or actual submissions.
